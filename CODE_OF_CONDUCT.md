# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment and sharing another
person's private information without consent are not acceptable.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
official project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict
participation or ban contributors when necessary, with action proportionate to
the behavior and its impact.

Report concerns privately to [coste200910@gmail.com](mailto:coste200910@gmail.com).
Share only information necessary to understand the concern. Reports will be
handled discreetly and shared only as needed to investigate or respond.
