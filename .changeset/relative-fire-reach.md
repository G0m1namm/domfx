Add creation-only Fire `flameReach`, a finite nonnegative fraction of the untransformed target border-box height. Relative mode keeps strength, reach and CSS-scale fine detail independent, resolves reach after resize, and reserves finite support under existing allocation gates. Zero reach/intensity is transparent. Legacy defaults and `flameHeight` behavior remain unchanged; combining both options is rejected. Relative mode requires contained paint without shadows, filters, outlines or generated pseudo-elements; insufficient paint margin reports `missing-exterior`.
